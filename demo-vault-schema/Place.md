# Place

Represents one geographic place, region, city, or residence.

## Permitted Relationships

- A Place may be located in another [Place](Place.md "{type: located_in}").
- A Place may be the residence of a [Person](Person.md "{type: residence_of, from: 0}").
- A Place may link to a related [Event](Event.md) through the bundle's explicit untyped OKF compatibility example.
